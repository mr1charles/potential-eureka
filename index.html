Linear functions

~ 12 MIN
THE CORE IDEA

A linear function describes a relationship with a constant rate of change.

Every time x changes by one unit, y changes by the same amount. That steady change is the slope.

y = mx + b m = slope    b = starting value
CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING01 / 03

A line passes through (0, 3) and rises 2 units for every 1 unit it moves right. What is its equation?

Exactly right. The slope is 2 and the y-intercept is 3. You’ve identified both pieces of the equation.
